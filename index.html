How it works

Two readings of the same land.

Condition and change are different questions. ClimaIQ answers each with the observation suited to it.

01 · CONDITION

The archive predicts where it stands.

Google's AlphaEarth embeddings — a learned summary of the accumulated satellite record — estimate current condition: bare ground, shrub and tree canopy, herbaceous cover, total vegetation. Wall-to-wall at 10 meter scale, including everywhere field crews can't reach, each prediction confined to a stated applicability domain.

02 · CHANGE

The radar catches what moved.

NASA's NISAR L-band radar, at 10 meter scale and revisiting every site every 12 days, detects change between field visits — treatment, fire, or moisture — on observations the annual record can't yet see.
